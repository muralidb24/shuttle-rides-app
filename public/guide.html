Ride, please!

A quick guide to requesting and giving rides to your community's destinations.

1 Sign in

Enter your email on the sign-in screen. You'll get an email with a link — click it and you're in, no password needed.

2 Join or create your community

The first time you sign in, you'll be asked whether you're joining an existing community or starting a new one. If someone shares a community code with you, enter it to join their group — you'll see the community's name to confirm before you're in. If you're the first person setting this up for your neighborhood, create a new community instead: pick a name and a code, and share that code with your neighbors so they can join. Whoever creates a community becomes its first admin.

Last step: tell us what to call you.

3 Keep an eye on the bell

The bell icon at the top of your dashboard is where in-app alerts show up - a new ride request, someone accepting your request, a ride getting cancelled, or "already covered" notices when another neighbor beat you to a request. A number badge shows how many are unread. Tap the bell to see them; each one has its own Acknowledge button, so you clear alerts one at a time rather than all at once - handy if a few come in and you want to deal with them individually. Most of these also go out as email, so the bell isn't the only way to catch them, but it's the quickest place to check what's new.

4 Request a ride

Tap "Request a ride" from your dashboard. Choose whether you're traveling out (need a ride to your destination) or returning (need a ride from your destination), pick the destination (if your community has more than one, or you'd like to change it from your default), then enter the date and pickup time. By default, every neighbor gets notified - see the next step if you'd rather limit that.

5 Set a default destination (optional)

If your community uses more than one destination, open your profile menu and tap "Default destination" to pick the one that should be pre-selected whenever you request a ride. You can still switch to a different destination on any individual request. Choose "None - ask me every time" if you'd rather always pick manually.

6 Choose who sees your requests (optional)

Open your profile menu (top right) and tap "Who sees my requests" to control the audience for every ride request you create from then on: everyone (the default), everyone except some neighbors you pick, or only some neighbors you pick. You can change this setting any time, and it applies going forward, not to requests you've already sent.

7 Turn on calendar sync (optional)

Tap "Calendar sync" on your dashboard and paste in your calendar's private feed URL, so the app can check your availability automatically and skip asking you for rides when you're already busy.

Google Calendar: Settings → pick your calendar → "Integrate calendar" → copy the "Secret address in iCal format."

Outlook / Outlook.com: Settings → Calendar → "Shared calendars" → publish a calendar and copy the ICS link.

Apple Calendar (iCloud): Right-click the calendar → Share Calendar → check "Public Calendar" → copy the link, changing webcal:// to https://.

Skip this and you'll simply be asked directly, every time a neighbor needs a ride.

8 Offer to give a ride

When a neighbor needs a ride, you'll see it under "Needs your response" on your dashboard, and get an email too - the destination is shown right there so you know where you're headed. If you've turned on calendar sync, we only ask when you're free — just tap "Offer to give a ride." Otherwise you'll be asked if you're available and willing. You also won't be asked if you're already giving another ride within about two hours of that time.

9 Add it to your calendar

Once you commit to a ride, we'll offer to add it to your calendar, destination included. If you'd rather not, we'll email you a reminder the day before and the day of instead.

10 Track and cancel rides

Your dashboard has two tabs: Committed (rides you're giving) and Requested (rides you've asked for) - each ride card shows its destination. Plans change — either side can cancel a ride any time from these lists. Unmatched ride requests also clean themselves up automatically an hour after the requested pickup time.

11 Community settings

Every member is an admin, so you'll always see a "Community settings" option in your profile menu - there's no single person the whole neighborhood depends on to keep things running. From there you can rename the community, change its join code, and manage the list of destinations members can request rides to or from (add, rename, or delete one - a destination can't be deleted while an active ride request still uses it). You can also remove another member's admin access if you'd ever want to (the app won't let the last remaining admin in a community be removed, so it's never possible to lock everyone out).
